# NeroDecor Wiki

Player- and contributor-facing documentation for **NeroDecor**, part of the Neroland ecosystem.
Built on **Neroland Core**.

> **Status:** beta — version `0.8.0-beta.1` "Chroma". Paintable hull, panel and glass families, the Luminous
> collection, 106 new blocks in ten collections, and the Chroma system: hubs that colour and animate linked
> lights. Keep this wiki updated alongside code changes (see [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) /
> [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md)).

## Start here

- [Chroma Guide](Chroma-Guide.md) — hub, linker, zones, colours, scenes, triggers, the editor, Scene Chips
  and share codes, the NeroLink remote, privacy and photosensitivity, in order.
- [Showroom](Showroom.md) — `/nerodecor gallery`: eight staged bays that show every collection in use.

## Collections

- [Chroma Lights](Chroma-Lights.md) — twelve hub-coloured fittings: strips, trims, tiles, pillars, tubes,
  panes, sconces, rings and lamps.
- [Bulkhead](Bulkhead.md) — sliding, iris and blast doors, trapdoors, airlock frames, viewports and smart
  glass.
- [Holo](Holo.md) — projectors, holo signs, arrow signs, world globes, holo tables, curtains, plinths and
  ticker strips.
- [Command](Command.md) — consoles, wall screens, racks, patch panels and the operator chair.
- [Lab & Habitat](Lab-And-Habitat.md) — benches, fume hoods, tanks, fridges, stools, bunks, lockers and
  planters.
- [Gantry](Gantry.md) — catwalks, railings, ladders, grates, struts and trusses.
- [Conduits](Conduits.md) — pipes, valves, gauges, cable trays, ducts and coolant tubes.
- [Seven Worlds](Seven-Worlds.md) — three or four blocks for each Nerospace world.
- [Ancients](Ancients.md) — glyph blocks, ancient brick, pillars, dormant cores and arch lintels.
- [Greebles](Greebles.md) — vents, panels, hazard stripes, deck signs, placards, rivets, brackets and floor
  arrows.
- [Luminous Collection](Luminous-Collection.md) — the glowing, animated, connected signature set
  (circuit plating, holo-grid floor, starfield, aurora glass, void rift, plasma conduit, fusion lamp
  and more).
