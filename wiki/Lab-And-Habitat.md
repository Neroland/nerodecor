# Lab & Habitat

Lab furniture and crew quarters.

| Block | What it is | Placement and properties | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lab Bench | A workbench | Runs along x or z; legs only at the ends of a run | - |
| Fume Hood | A hood with a glass sash | Faces you | Use raises and lowers the sash |
| Specimen Tank | A glass tank with bubbling fluid | Stacks join into one tall tank; Chroma tints the fluid | - |
| Sample Fridge | A cabinet with twin glass doors | Faces you | Use opens and closes it |
| Lab Stool | A padded stool | No properties | Empty hand: sit down |
| Crew Bunk | A two-block bunk | Placed like a bed (foot, then head) | Cosmetic: it does not set your spawn or skip the night |
| Locker | A tall locker with twin doors | Faces you; stacks join | Use opens and closes it |
| Planter | A trough with a plant | Runs along x or z; four plants: fern, violet bloom, ember succulent, frost reed | Empty hand: next plant |

## Using them

- Fridges, lockers and the fume hood are decorative: they hold no items.
- Lab stools and operator chairs use the same seat: sneak to stand up.

## Recipes

Iron, glass and quartz; the bunk is made from a bed, the locker from a chest and the planter from dirt. Full
