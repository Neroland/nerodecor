# Recipes (0.8.0)

Every 0.8.0 block and Chroma tool is craftable from vanilla materials, plus Neroland Core's Nero Alloy for the
Blast Door. Nothing is gated: no other mod and no progression step is needed. The Seven Worlds blocks use vanilla
stand-ins for each world's rock, so they need no Nerospace install.

- Each recipe unlocks in the recipe book the first time you hold one of its ingredients.
- Plain tiles and trim make 4 to 8 per craft, so decorating in bulk stays cheap.
- Each Seven Worlds world has one crafted base block; its other shapes come off the stonecutter one for one.
  The stonecutter also cuts slabs (two per block) and the Ancients shapes.
- Recipe viewers (JEI, EMI) show an information page for the Chroma Hub, Chroma Linker, Paint Gun and Scene
  Chip. The legacy neon items are hidden there.

The Chroma Cube (4 glass around 1 glowstone dust makes 4) and the Paint Gun keep their recipes from
[Chroma Cube, Paint Gun and the neon fold](Chroma-Cube-And-Paint-Gun.md). Many Chroma Lights start from Chroma Cubes.
