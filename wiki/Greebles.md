# Greebles

Small surface details that make a base look lived in. All of them mount on a face of their own block space
(on the floor, a wall or the ceiling) and are mostly thin plates.

| Block | What it is | Placement and properties | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Louvre Vent | A wall louvre grille | Mounts on any face | - |
| Maintenance Panel | A cover plate | Mounts on any face | Empty hand: open to show the wiring |
| Hazard Stripe | A yellow and black decal | Mounts on any face; joins end to end | Empty hand: switch between a full stripe and a 4 px edge border |
| Deck Sign | A lit deck number | Mounts on any face; numbers 0 to 9 | Empty hand: next number |
| Warning Placard | A warning sign | Mounts on any face; caution, voltage, radiation, flammable, cryo, suit, no entry, oxygen | Empty hand: next symbol |
| Rivet Plate | A riveted 1 px plate | Mounts on any face; joins plates on the same face | - |
| Pipe Bracket | A clamp sized for decor pipes | Mounts on any face; along an axis | - |
| Floor Arrow | A painted arrow with a faint glow | Lies on the floor; points in eight directions | Empty hand: turn it |

## Recipes

Iron nuggets and dyes, mostly 4 to 8 per craft. Full list: [Recipes](Recipes.md#greebles).
